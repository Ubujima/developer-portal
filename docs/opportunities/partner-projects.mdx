# Partner Projects

Ubujima collaborates with selected founders, organizations, agencies, and communities working on technology projects that may benefit from developer collaboration.

Partner projects give developers opportunities to apply their skills to real initiatives beyond Ubujima's own products.

Projects may involve:

- Open-source development
- Product development
- Technical research
- Testing
- Documentation
- UI/UX
- Infrastructure
- AI
- Community technology

Each project will define its own scope, contribution model, licensing terms, ownership arrangements, and participation requirements.

## Available Projects

Partner projects will be published here as they become available.
