# The Ubujima Ecosystem

Ubujima is developing an interconnected technology ecosystem rather than a collection of isolated products.

## 🌍 AfroCom

AfroCom is the community-facing platform and the first major product within the ecosystem.

Today, its core focus is community networking. Over time, AfroCom is intended to expand into a broader platform combining areas such as:

- Community networking
- Business and creator tools
- Marketplace and opportunity discovery
- Events and projects
- Communication
- Entertainment
- Crowdfunding
- Utilities
- Financial services
- AI-powered experiences
- Blockchain-enabled services where appropriate

## 🤖 Ma'at AI

Ma'at AI is Ubujima's artificial intelligence initiative.

Its potential applications include:

- Intelligent user experiences
- Personalized recommendations
- Intelligent matching
- Well-being services
- Collective intelligence
- Business intelligence
- AI-powered productivity tools
- Data and knowledge services

Ma'at AI may support AfroCom while also developing into a reusable intelligence layer for other products and services.

## 🔗 Maji

Maji is Ubujima's blockchain and decentralized-infrastructure initiative.

The project is exploring capabilities such as:

- Digital ownership
- Wallet and transaction infrastructure
- Crowdfunding
- Community governance
- Decentralized participation
- Identity and trust

Maji remains an evolving initiative, with implementation guided by real product and community needs.

## 🧱 Shared Platform Direction

As the ecosystem grows, common platform capabilities can support multiple products without requiring every product to rebuild the same functionality.

These capabilities may include areas such as:

- Identity and access
- APIs and service interfaces
- Data services
- Communication
- Notifications
- Payments
- Security
- Observability
- Developer tooling

Our architectural direction is toward a **modular, API-first and service-oriented ecosystem** in which capabilities can evolve independently while remaining interoperable.

The public Developer Hub describes this direction at a high level. Detailed implementation information is provided to contributors when it is relevant to their work.

## 🚀 Build the Ecosystem

Developers may contribute to individual projects while keeping the broader ecosystem in mind.

The objective is not simply to build more software.

**The objective is to build technology that works together.**
