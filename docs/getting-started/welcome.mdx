# Welcome to Ubujima Developer Hub

**Build the Technology. Shape the Communities.**

Ubujima Corporation is building an interconnected technology ecosystem designed to create new possibilities for communities, creators, businesses, organizations and individuals across the global Afro-community and beyond.

Our technology roadmap brings together **community networking, artificial intelligence, blockchain, commerce, financial technology, entertainment, collaboration and digital infrastructure**.

This Developer Hub explains the products we are building, the engineering direction behind them, and how developers and technical contributors can participate.

## Our Ecosystem

### 🌍 AfroCom

AfroCom is the community layer of the Ubujima ecosystem.

It is evolving from a community networking platform toward a broader digital ecosystem connecting people, communities, creators, businesses and organizations.

### 🤖 Ma'at AI

Ma'at AI is our artificial intelligence initiative.

It explores intelligent services, personalization, collective intelligence, well-being and AI-powered tools that can support AfroCom and other parts of the Ubujima ecosystem.

### 🔗 Maji

Maji is our blockchain and decentralized-infrastructure initiative.

It explores technology for trusted participation, ownership, transactions, governance and community-powered economic activity where decentralized approaches create meaningful value.

## 🛠️ Build With Us

Ubujima is being built progressively through internal development, open-source collaboration, community co-creation and selected partnerships.

We welcome developers, architects, designers, AI engineers, blockchain developers, researchers, DevOps engineers, security specialists and other technical contributors who want to participate in the journey.

Some projects and repositories are public, while other areas remain private because of security, intellectual property, commercial considerations or product maturity.

Technical details, development environments and implementation resources are shared according to the needs of each project and contributor role.

## 🚀 Where to Start

If you are new to Ubujima, we recommend this path:

1. **Learn about Ubujima**
2. **Explore the ecosystem**
3. **Understand our engineering direction**
4. **Review our contribution process**
5. **Choose a project, challenge or issue**
6. **Join the relevant contributor community**
7. **Complete any project-specific onboarding where required**

Our goal is simple:

> **Build useful technology together, openly where possible, and responsibly where necessary.**

Welcome to the journey.
