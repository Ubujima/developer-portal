# Welcome to Ubujima Developer Hub

**Build the Technology. Shape the Communities.**

Ubujima Corporation is building an interconnected technology ecosystem designed to create new possibilities for communities, creators, businesses, organizations and individuals across the global Afro-community and beyond.

Our technology roadmap brings together **community networking, artificial intelligence, blockchain, commerce, financial technology, entertainment, collaboration and digital infrastructure**.

This Developer Hub is where we document how the ecosystem is built and how developers and technical contributors can participate.

## Our Ecosystem

### 🌍 AfroCom

AfroCom is the community layer of the Ubujima ecosystem.

It is evolving from a community networking platform into a broader super-app ecosystem connecting people, communities, creators, businesses and organizations.

### 🤖 Ma'at AI

Ma'at AI is our artificial intelligence initiative.

It explores intelligent services, personalization, collective intelligence, well-being and AI-powered tools that can become part of AfroCom and other parts of the Ubujima ecosystem.

### 🔗 Maji Blockchain

Maji is our blockchain initiative.

It is being developed as a potential infrastructure layer for decentralized identity, transactions, ownership, governance and community-powered economic activity.

## 🛠️ Build With Us

Ubujima is being built progressively.

We welcome developers, architects, designers, AI engineers, blockchain developers, researchers, DevOps engineers, security specialists and other technical contributors who want to participate in the journey.

Some projects and repositories are open source, while other parts of the ecosystem remain private during development.

Access to private repositories is granted based on project needs, contribution areas and appropriate onboarding.

## 🚀 Where to Start

If you are new to Ubujima, we recommend following this path:

1. **Learn about Ubujima**
2. **Explore the ecosystem**
3. **Understand the current technology stack**
4. **Review our contribution process**
5. **Choose a project or area where you can contribute**
6. **Join the relevant developer community**
7. **Complete onboarding and verification where required**

Our goal is simple:

> **Build useful technology together, openly where possible, and responsibly where necessary.**

Welcome to the journey.
