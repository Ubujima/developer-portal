# About Ubujima

**Ubujima Corporation is building technology for shared prosperity.**

Ubujima is a technology company building an interconnected ecosystem of products and infrastructure designed to empower people, communities, creators, businesses and organizations.

Our work focuses particularly on the global Afro-community while building technology with broader applications and potential.

## What We're Building

Our ecosystem brings together several technology layers:

- 🌍 **Community** — connecting people, communities, creators and organizations.
- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence** — intelligent tools, personalization and collective intelligence.
- 🔗 **Blockchain** — decentralized infrastructure, ownership, transactions and governance.
- 🛍️ **Commerce** — marketplaces, business tools and digital transactions.
- 💳 **Financial Technology** — wallets, payments, crowdfunding and related financial services.
- 🎬 **Entertainment** — music, movies, games and other digital experiences.
- 🧩 **Collaboration** — tools for co-creation and community participation.

## Our Approach

We believe meaningful technology should be built with the communities it serves.

That means combining engineering and research with community participation, partnerships, experimentation and open collaboration where appropriate.

Some parts of the ecosystem will be developed openly through open-source projects and contributor communities. Other components may remain private where security, intellectual property, commercial considerations or product maturity require it.

## Why Developers Matter

Ubujima is not being built by a single team working in isolation.

We are developing an ecosystem that can benefit from contributions across software engineering, AI, blockchain, infrastructure, security, design, research, data and community technology.

The Developer Hub exists to make that collaboration easier.
