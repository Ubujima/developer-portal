# 🤖 Ma'at AI

**Intelligence for the Ubujima ecosystem.**

Ma'at AI is Ubujima's artificial intelligence initiative, being developed alongside AfroCom.

The project explores how AI can create greater value for users, communities, creators, businesses and organizations.

## Areas of Exploration

Ma'at AI is expected to explore capabilities such as:

- 🧠 Intelligent user experiences
- 🎯 Personalization
- 🤝 Intelligent matching
- 🌱 Well-being assistance
- 🧩 Collective intelligence
- 📊 Business intelligence
- ✍️ AI-assisted creation
- 🔎 Intelligent discovery
- 📚 Knowledge and information services

## Relationship with AfroCom

AI will progressively become part of the AfroCom experience.

Potential applications include:

- Personalized content
- User and community discovery
- Intelligent recommendations
- Well-being assistance
- AI-powered search
- Creator and business tools
- Community intelligence
- Productivity tools

## Development Direction

Ma'at AI is being developed as more than a single feature.

The long-term vision is for it to become a reusable intelligence layer that can support multiple Ubujima products and services.

The project may therefore include:

- AI services
- Model integrations
- Data pipelines
- Knowledge systems
- AI agents
- APIs
- Evaluation systems
- Safety and governance mechanisms

## Building the Team

We are progressively building a technical and research community around Ma'at AI.

Potential contributors include:

- AI/ML engineers
- Data engineers
- Researchers
- Backend engineers
- AI product developers
- Security specialists
- Domain experts

**The goal is responsible intelligence that creates practical value.**
