# 🔗 Maji Blockchain

**Exploring decentralized infrastructure for the Ubujima ecosystem.**

Maji is Ubujima's blockchain initiative.

The project is currently being researched and developed as a potential infrastructure layer for decentralized participation, ownership, transactions and governance.

## Areas of Exploration

Maji may support areas such as:

- 💳 Digital wallets
- 🔄 Transactions
- 🤝 Crowdfunding
- 🏛️ Decentralized governance
- 🪪 Digital identity
- 🔐 Trust and verification
- 🏦 Financial technology
- 🌍 Community-powered economic participation

## Relationship with AfroCom

The long-term vision is for blockchain capabilities to become integrated into AfroCom where they provide genuine user value.

Potential applications include:

- AfroCom wallet
- Community crowdfunding
- Digital ownership
- Creator economics
- Business transactions
- Community governance
- Decentralized participation

## Current Status

Maji is currently in the research and architecture phase.

A technical community is being developed around the project, alongside the continued development of its technical and economic foundations.

## Potential Contributors

We are interested in contributors with experience in:

- Blockchain engineering
- Smart contracts
- Distributed systems
- Cryptography
- Web3 infrastructure
- Wallet technology
- Security
- Token economics
- Governance systems

**Decentralization should solve real problems—not simply add a blockchain.**
