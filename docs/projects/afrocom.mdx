# 🌍 AfroCom

**The community layer of the Ubujima ecosystem.**

AfroCom is a community-driven platform designed to connect people, communities, creators, businesses and organizations across the global Afro-community.

It is the first major product being developed within the Ubujima ecosystem.

## Current Focus

AfroCom currently operates primarily as a **community networking and opportunity platform**.

The immediate product focus is to strengthen the core community experience while expanding the web experience and preparing the platform for a more modular architecture.

## Current Platform Capabilities

AfroCom is progressively developing capabilities including:

- 👥 Community networking
- 📝 Posts and content
- 💬 Communication
- 📍 Location and discovery
- 🏢 Business and organization profiles
- 📅 Events
- 💼 Jobs and opportunities
- 📣 Campaigns
- 🤝 Community co-creation
- 🎥 Rich media

## Current Development Priorities

Our immediate priorities include:

- Stabilizing and improving existing community features
- Improving UI and UX across devices
- Improving performance, reliability and accessibility
- Strengthening discovery and opportunity experiences
- Expanding web capabilities
- Improving testing and development workflows
- Establishing clearer service boundaries and APIs
- Preparing the platform for progressive architectural expansion

## Architecture Mission

AfroCom is evolving toward a **modular, API-first, service-oriented architecture**.

The objective is not to create complexity for its own sake or to split every function into a separate service immediately.

Instead, we aim to establish clear product domains and introduce independently deployable services where scale, maintainability, ownership or product requirements justify them.

The first architectural focus remains the community-networking experience that exists today. Future services can then be introduced progressively as the product expands.

Potential platform domains include:

- Identity and access
- Profiles and social relationships
- Content and community interaction
- Discovery and search
- Communication
- Notifications
- Organizations and opportunities
- Media
- Future commerce and platform services

Detailed implementation decisions are maintained within the relevant project and contributor environments rather than the public project overview.

## The Next Evolution

AfroCom is intended to evolve beyond community networking into a broader digital ecosystem.

Longer-term areas may include:

- 🛍️ Marketplace
- 💳 Financial services and wallet experiences
- 🎬 Entertainment
- 🎮 Games
- 🎵 Music
- 🎥 Video and streaming
- 🤝 Crowdfunding
- 🧩 Co-creation
- 🛠️ Utilities
- 🤖 AI-powered services
- 🔗 Blockchain-enabled services

This evolution will happen progressively, guided by user needs, technical readiness and sustainable architecture.

**Build what creates value today. Architect for what comes next.**
